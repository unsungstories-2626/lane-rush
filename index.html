1Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Lane Rush (package com.codebyrushi.lanerush), published by CodeByRushi ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the Lane Rush app for Android only.

2Summary

Lane Rush has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, location or any other personal information. Your game progress is stored on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob; the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app collects and shares certain device and usage data with Google to serve and measure ads, as described below.

3Game data stored on your device

The game saves your scores, coins, coin history, unlocked cars, level stars, mission and daily-reward progress, gameplay statistics and settings (sound, music and effects volume, vibration, speedometer) in the app's private storage on your device. We (the developer) do not receive this data; the app has no server of its own.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this game data in your device backup to your Google account and restore it on a new device. This is controlled by your device settings and is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

4Advertising (Google AdMob)

Lane Rush is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob: a banner below the play area, occasional full-screen ads between runs, and optional rewarded ads (to continue a run or double coins).

To request, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived);
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads and app diagnostics (for example ad impressions, taps, crashes and performance).

All of this is processed by Google, not by us. We do not see your advertising ID and we do not combine ad data with any other information.

Google's practices: policies.google.com/privacy and How Google uses information from partner sites and apps.
AdMob data disclosure: Google Play data disclosure for the Mobile Ads SDK.

5Your consent and ad choices

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are personalised, and does not request ads until a consent choice allows it. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → "Privacy & ad choices" when that option is shown for your region.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

6Permissions

Lane Rush does not request any runtime ("dangerous") permission: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, photos/media or file access, and the game never shows a permission prompt.

The app declares only normal permissions, which Android grants at install without a prompt:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and the consent form. The game itself is fully playable offline.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and Android ad-services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, _ATTRIBUTION, _TOPICS): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to read the advertising ID and to use Android's Privacy Sandbox ad APIs where your device supports them and your settings allow it.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by Google libraries bundled with the ads SDK (AndroidX WorkManager) for short background tasks of the SDK. Lane Rush itself runs no background or foreground service.

7Diagnostics and analytics

Lane Rush does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service (such as Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics). If you have opted in to sharing usage and diagnostics with Google or the Play Store on your device, Google may provide us with aggregated, anonymous crash and performance statistics through the Google Play Console.

8Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to answer you. The "Contact developer" button pre-fills the app version and device model so we can help; you can delete these lines before sending. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your request.

9Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data (Android Settings → Apps → Lane Rush → Storage → Clear data). Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID or manage it at myadcenter.google.com. To ask us to delete a support conversation, email us.

10Children's privacy

Lane Rush is a casual game for a general audience and is not directed primarily at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by Google AdMob under its policies. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

11Third-party services

The only third-party services in the app are Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform, both provided by Google LLC. Links to the Google Play Store (Rate / Share) open Google Play, which is covered by Google's privacy policy.

12Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Ad traffic handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum.

Help

How do I collect coins?

Gold coins appear only in the three driving lanes. Drive through a coin to collect it. Each coin adds to your score, charges a little nitro, and is added to your wallet when it is saved. Tap the coin counter on the home screen to see your coin history.

How does nitro work?

Tap the NITRO button (bottom right) when the ring is charged. While boosting, the car is faster and knocks traffic aside. Nitro charges over time, from coins, near misses and red fuel cans.

How do levels work?

Each level has goals such as a distance, coins or near misses. Finish the goals to earn up to three stars and coins. Stars unlock the following levels.

What are missions and awards?

Three daily missions are picked each day and reset at local midnight. Awards are permanent challenges. When a mission or award is complete, tap CLAIM to collect its coins.

How do I get new cars?

Spend coins on new cars in the Garage. Each car has its own perk. Purchases appear in your coin history.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it, unless Android has restored it from your device backup.
